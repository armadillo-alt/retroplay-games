Keys and what the numbers mean

Keyboard or TV remote: arrows move, Enter is Start, Z is B, X is A, A is Y, S is X, Q and W are the shoulder buttons, Shift is Select.

The speed test runs the emulator flat out for 10 seconds without drawing, then reports how many times faster than a real console this device is. 100% is just enough; 150% or more leaves room for the picture, the sound and busier games.

The test cartridge was written for Retroplay: it switches on every picture layer and keeps the processor busy, which is about as heavy as an ordinary game. Chips inside some cartridges (Super FX, SA-1) make those games heavier.
